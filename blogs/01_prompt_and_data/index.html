The answer is the suffix k] after the prefill. Yes/no tasks use [1]/[2] too. Rendered with Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B's chat template.

Data

  • 12 datasets, 1,000 training rows each: 3 intent, 6 short reasoning, 3 long-input (Reason+Long-12k).
  • Options are shuffled per example at training time, so gold lands on any [k].
  • Rows store parts, question and options separately; the prompt is rendered from them.
  • State parts carry no names, are separated by a blank line, and the question always sits just before Options:.
  • Inputs are never truncated; rows are capped at 10,240 tokens.
  • ProofWriter, ContractNLI and MuSiQue rows keep native evidence spans; all others have none.
